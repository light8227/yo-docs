# Modding Guides

Here are a collection of guides for modding the Yo-kai Watch games. For all guides, similar to other resources on the site, assume the guide is intended for the 3DS Yo-kai Watch games unless stated otherwise. Additionally, by 3DS Yo-kai Watch games, I do not include Yo-kai Sangokushi as it does not use Level5's engine. Unless specified, a guide may not work for any remakes/remasters. If any guides are wrong, please feel free to contact @n123original on discord, or make an Issue on the site's github if you do not have discord, although the former is preferred.
