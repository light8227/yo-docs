# Modding Guides

Here are a collection of guides for modding the Yo-kai Watch games. For all guides, similar to the other resources hosted on this site, assume the guide is intended for the 3DS Yo-kai Watch games unless stated otherwise. Additionally, by 3DS Yo-kai Watch games, I do not include Yo-kai Sangokushi as it does not use Level5's engine. Unless specified, a guide may not work for any remakes/remasters. If any guides are wrong, please feel free to contact @n123original on Discord, or make an Issue on the site's Github if you do not have Discord, although the former is preferred, and will be likely be seen earlier, therefore yielding a faster response
